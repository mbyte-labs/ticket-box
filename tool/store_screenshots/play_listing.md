## App name

Ticket Box

## Short description (80 max)

Track your shows and movies on your phone. No account, no ads, data stays local.

## Full description (4000 max)

Ticket Box keeps track of what you watch, what comes next and where to watch it, all on your phone. No sign-up, no ads, no subscription. Your library lives on your device.

UP NEXT TO WATCH
Open the app and see the next unwatched episode of every show you follow, with season premiere and finale badges. Mark it watched or check in with one tap, and the next one takes its place.

SHOW PROGRESS
A progress bar for every show, broken down per season, with the last episode you watched and when. Finished shows keep a full bar; the ones you gave up on can be dropped out of the way.

LISTS
A watchlist for movies and shows you still want to see, favorites for the ones you love, and a dropped list for shows you gave up on so they stop showing up in Up Next.

DISCOVER AND SEARCH
Browse popular, top rated and trending movies and shows, filtered by rating, release year and genre, or search TMDB's whole catalogue by title.

DETAILS THAT MATTER
Every movie, show, season and episode page has the overview, TMDB and IMDb ratings, the trailer, the cast with their other work, and a Where to watch row with the streaming, rental and purchase services in your country.

WATCH HISTORY
Mark anything watched with today's date, its release date or a date you pick. Filter your history by movies or episodes and by how it was logged, and rate everything on a 1 to 10 scale.

STATS
Hours watched, plays, movies versus TV, your most watched show and how you hand out ratings, per year and all time.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Everything is stored locally. Export your history, ratings, watchlist and favorites to a JSON file whenever you like and restore it on a new phone. Optionally log in with a TMDB account to mirror your ratings, watchlist and favorites there. Your watch history never leaves the device.

FREE, NO ADS
Ticket Box is free and shows no ads. If it earns a place on your home screen, there is a "buy me a coffee" tip inside, but every feature works the same either way.

Metadata and artwork come from The Movie Database. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch.
