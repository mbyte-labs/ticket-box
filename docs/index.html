Ticket Box

Track your shows and movies. Free, with no ads.

Ticket Box keeps track of what you watch, what comes next and where to watch it, on your Android phone.

Free, and it stays that way

Ticket Box is a personal project that has been on Google Play since 2021. It costs nothing and shows no ads.

No ads, no subscription

Every feature is free. There is nothing to unlock and nothing to pay for.

No account needed

Install it and start tracking. There is no sign-up and no login to get started.

Your data, your device

Your library is stored on your phone and works offline. Export it to a file and restore it on another device.

What it does

Everything you need to follow your shows and remember the movies you have seen.

Up next to watch

The next unwatched episode of every show you follow, with season premiere and finale badges.

Show progress

A progress bar for every show, per season, with the last episode you watched and when.

Lists

A watchlist for what you want to see, favorites for what you love, and a dropped list for shows you gave up on.

Discover and search

Browse popular, top rated and trending titles, filter by rating, year and genre, or search the whole TMDB catalogue.

Details

Overview, TMDB and IMDb ratings, trailer, cast, and where to stream, rent or buy in your country.

Watch history

Log anything with today's date, its release date or a date you pick, and rate it from 1 to 10.

Stats

Hours watched, plays, movies versus TV and your most watched show, per year and all time.

Optional TMDB login

Log in with a TMDB account to mirror your ratings, watchlist and favorites. Your watch history never leaves the device.
