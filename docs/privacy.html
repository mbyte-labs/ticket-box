Ticket Box

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: Ticket Box has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it needs no account. Your library, watch history and ratings are stored on your device. The developer does not run a server and never receives your data.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Ticket Box app for Android (package ro.madcodestudios.ticket.box), published on Google Play. Ticket Box is an open source project; its source code is at github.com/mbyte-labs/ticket-box.

Data stored on your device

Everything you do in the app is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your library of movies and shows, your watch history, ratings, watchlist, favorites and dropped shows;
  • your app settings;
  • a copy of the public information and artwork of the titles in your library, so the app works offline;
  • technical log files that help diagnose problems.

None of this is sent to the developer. The log files leave your device only if you choose to export and share them yourself.

Data sent to third-party services

To show information about movies and shows, the app contacts these services directly from your device:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB), for titles, artwork, cast, trailers and streaming availability. Requests contain what you search or browse for, the identifiers of the titles involved, and your language and region settings. See the TMDB privacy policy.
  • OMDb, for IMDb scores. Requests contain the identifier of the title. See OMDb's terms.

Like any internet request, these reach the service together with your IP address. They are made with the app's own access keys and carry no name, email address, device identifier or advertising identifier. Your watch history is never part of them.

Optional TMDB account

You can use Ticket Box without any account. If you choose to log in with a TMDB account in Settings:

  • you sign in and approve access on the TMDB website; the app never sees your TMDB password;
  • the app stores a TMDB session identifier on your device;
  • your ratings, watchlist and favorites are copied to and from your TMDB account, where TMDB's own privacy policy applies;
  • your watch history is not sent to TMDB.

Logging out in Settings removes the session from your device and stops the copying. Data already in your TMDB account stays there until you remove it on TMDB.

Donations

Ticket Box is free. Optional donations are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. The app does not receive or store your payment details, and it keeps no record of who donated. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google privacy policy.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting service, and it does not use the Android advertising ID.

Backups and export

  • You can export your library to a JSON file and restore it later or on another device. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it.
  • If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in the backup of your Google account. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google, not by the developer.

Permissions

  • Internet, to reach the services listed above.
  • Google Play Billing, for optional donations.
  • Calendar: declared by the app for an earlier feature. The current version does not ask for this permission and does not read or write your calendar.

Links to other sites

Links in the app, such as trailers on YouTube, IMDb pages or the app's community pages, open in your browser or in the matching app. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Ticket Box does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because your data is on your device, you are in control of it. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. Ratings, watchlist and favorites copied to a TMDB account can be removed on TMDB.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/mbyte-labs/ticket-box/issues.
